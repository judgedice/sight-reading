Match a note: states

One note, a needle you can read from across the room. The band is the in-tune window at difficulty 1 (voice ±25 cents). Low and high are said in words, with the chevron on the needle's side, never red: this is a warm-up, not a grade. A hit needs 0.8 s inside the band. In a round the next note comes 1.5 s after a hit; a note chosen from the range chips stays until another is chosen.
