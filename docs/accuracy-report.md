## Per-instrument gates (01 §13.2)

Thresholds: onset F1 ≥ 0.97 at ±30 ms (room: ≥ 0.93, PLAN R45); judgment agreement ≥ 95%; false missed ≤ 1%; false extras ≤ 1 per take; zero click-induced extras; live vs final divergence ≤ 3%; pitch-class accuracy ≥ 99%; octave errors ≤ 0.5%; live sharp/flat on in-tune notes ≤ 1% (PLAN R19); c90 ≤ 5 c on clean capture (01 §8.4: non-vibrato notes with ≥ 250 ms steady, vibrato notes with ≥ 360 ms steady). Bold cells with ✗ fail. The room condition is reported in full but gated only on F1 (R45).

Definitions. Onset F1: one-to-one greedy matching of detected onsets to the detectable truth onsets at ±30 ms (as the P4c and P4d gates). Verdict agreement: final status, timing verdict and pitch verdict (in tune and not graded count as one) against the truth verdict, over expected notes whose true timing error is more than 15 ms from tRed and the match window and whose true deviation is more than 5 c from redCents (closer than that the detector's own error decides; those notes are the "boundary" column). False missed: truly matched notes the final alignment calls missed. False extras: final extras with no played extra or displaced note within 60 ms; click-induced when within 25 ms of a bleed click with no played note within 80 ms. Live≠final: final judgments flagged `rejudged`. False red: notes played in tune (no injected intonation error, |true deviation| ≤ 10 c) that are eligible for a live mark and showed sharp or flat at any time during the take.
