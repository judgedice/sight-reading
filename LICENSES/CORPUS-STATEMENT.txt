Contributions of this project to the score corpus
=================================================

The excerpts in public/corpus/v1/ are cut, normalized and annotated from OpenScore Lieder and OpenScore String
Quartets, both released under CC0 1.0 Universal by their contributors. The cutting, the normalization, the
level and feature annotations and the review records that this project adds are released under the same
CC0 1.0 Universal dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): to the extent possible under
law, the authors of this project have waived all copyright and related or neighbouring rights to them.

The underlying compositions are in the public domain: every composer included died before 1931 (PLAN ruling 28).
Per-excerpt provenance (upstream repository, commit and path) is in each excerpt's `source` record.
